**Automatic reconnect follow-up**

Automatic retry was subsequently added to LobbyManager: delays are 2, 4, 8, 16 and then 30 seconds, repeating at the cap. Duplicate disconnect callbacks share a single coroutine. Successful connection cancels retries and resets the attempt counter. Intentional disconnect, shutdown and component disable stop retries. Offline mode does not retry. GameManager now routes unexpected match disconnects through the persistent return helper to Lobby, where connection recovery is handled; interrupted matches are not resumed.

VerifyAutoReconnect.Run passed with simulated timeout callbacks that started real Photon connection attempts after 2 and 4 seconds. Checks also covered the next 8-second delay, 30-second cap, duplicate-loop prevention, cancellation and realtime scheduling with gameplay time paused. VerifyAutoReconnect.MatchDisconnect passed the simulated match-disconnect return to Lobby. Compilation passed and final test console had zero errors/warnings. Successful online reconnection remains blocked by the connectivity/certificate failures above. Evidence: AgentScripts/AutoReconnect-validation.txt. The Windows build above predates this automatic-retry follow-up; current source contains it.
